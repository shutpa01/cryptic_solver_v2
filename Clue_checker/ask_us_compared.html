Support without knowing who you're talking to

How the Ask Us system compares with the closest products we found, on the things that matter when you deliberately hold no user data.

How Ask Us works

  1. The user types a question in the app. There's no name or email field.
  2. A Cloudflare Worker emails it to the support inbox with a ticket number.
  3. The device keeps the ticket number itself. The user never sees it.
  4. We reply from ordinary Gmail. The reply comes back through Cloudflare.
  5. The app shows "We've replied" the next time it opens.

One by one

Incogneato

~$17–$83 / month

An anonymous suggestion box with chat.

✕The user must keep a return code to see the reply. Lose it and the conversation is gone.
✓Ask Us: the device keeps the ticket, so there's nothing to lose.

Anonymeter

price not found

Anonymous feedback forms with follow-up questions.

~The reply appears when the user goes back to the form link. How it recognises them isn't documented.
✓Ask Us: the reply appears in the app they already use, with a notice.

Zendesk

~$55–$115 per agent / month

A full helpdesk.

✕Most anonymous requests now have to verify an email address, so you end up holding that address.
✕Priced per staff member, every month.
✓Ask Us: no email ever, and you answer from Gmail you already pay for.

Customer.io

from ~$100 / month

Marketing messages, including to anonymous visitors.

✕Built to send to users, not to hear from them.
✓Ask Us: a real two-way conversation, started by the user.

GhostChat

free, then ~$5–$25 / month

A cookie-free live chat widget.

~Live chat works while both sides are online. I found no documented way to reply to someone who has left.
✓Ask Us: answer within 24 hours, and the reply waits for them.

Where the others are ahead

  • Live, real-time chat: GhostChat, Incogneato.
  • Team features such as queues, assignment and reports: Zendesk.
  • Works on a website with no app: most of them. Ask Us is built into our app; a web version would keep the ticket in the browser instead.
  • If the user clears the app or changes phone, an unanswered ticket is lost.

Prices are from review sites (Capterra, SaaSWorthy, eesel) and vendor pages, checked October 2026, and may be out of date.
