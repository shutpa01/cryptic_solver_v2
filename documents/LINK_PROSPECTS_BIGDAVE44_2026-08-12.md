## Honest reading of this
- The headline "120 domains" is softer than it looks: 11.9K of BD's links are from his own
  second domain and 7.7K are blogspot.com — a platform aggregate, not one publisher.
- The genuinely pitchable list is roughly **20 crossword sites + 8 setter blogs**, not 120.
- theguardian.com → BD (23 links) is the standout authority link and worth understanding
  before anything else — likely from Guardian crossword blog posts.
- Caveat on the premise: these sites link to BD because he has published daily since 2009 and
  is a community institution. "No objection to linking to a beginners' site" is plausible but
  untested — nobody has been asked.
