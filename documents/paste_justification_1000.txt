Cordelia (justcordelia.com) explains cryptic crossword clues. It assembles a short video walkthrough of each day's puzzle and uploads it to one YouTube channel I own (UCPUPMydfeAAC7lFTPOMfRFw). It has no users but me; no site visitor signs in, and it acts only on my own channel.

youtube.upload - calls videos.insert to upload the assembled file with its title, description and tags. Uploading is the app's only purpose.

youtube.readonly - calls channels.list with mine=true at setup, reading only the channel id and title, to confirm the credential points at the intended channel. Without it the app cannot tell which channel it controls.

No narrower scope permits uploading a video, or reading back which channel a credential controls. Both replace the broader "youtube" scope, which the app requested before, never used, and removed. No other write method is called.

No other Google data is accessed, sold, transferred, or used for ads or AI training. See https://justcordelia.com/privacy
