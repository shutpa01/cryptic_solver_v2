Privacy Policy

justcordelia.com · last updated 21 August 2026

The short version

There are no accounts on this site. I never ask for your name, your email address or your payment details, and there is nowhere to enter them. I don't sell anything and I don't sell anyone's data.

What is collected

  • ● Your IP address, recorded briefly to count requests and stop one visitor hammering the site. It is used for rate limiting and nothing else.
  • ● A session cookie (cordelia_session), set when a page loads and valid for one hour. It carries no identity — it exists so that automated scrapers can't request answers without having loaded a page first.
  • ● Usage analytics, through Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. These tell me which pages get visited and where people get stuck. Clarity also records interactions such as clicks and scrolling so I can see where the site is confusing. Both set their own cookies and both are governed by their own privacy policies.
  • ● Standard server and network logs. Traffic passes through Cloudflare, which keeps its own logs to filter abuse.

Use of Google account data

Cordelia publishes a daily video walkthrough of a crossword to a YouTube channel that I own. To do that, the software signs in to a single Google account — my own — and requests permission to upload and manage videos on that one channel.

That permission is never requested from visitors to this site. No visitor signs in to anything here, and Cordelia has no access to any other person's Google account, YouTube channel or Google data.

The resulting credential is stored on my own machine, is not part of the website, and is not shared with anyone. It is used only to create videos on that channel, and can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings.

Cordelia's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, never transferred to third parties, and never used for advertising.

Crossword content

The clues and answers explained on this site come from published newspaper crosswords. Nothing you type into the pattern finder, anagram solver or search box is stored against you.

Children

This site is not directed at children and collects nothing that would identify anyone, of any age.

Getting in touch

Questions about this policy, or a request to remove something, go to justcordelia.com@gmail.com.
