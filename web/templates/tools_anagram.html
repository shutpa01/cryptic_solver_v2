Crossword anagram solver — common questions

How do I solve a crossword anagram?

Type the jumbled letters into the box and press Solve. I rearrange them and show only words that have actually appeared as crossword answers, so you don't have to wade through nonsense.

Can it solve multi-word answers?

Yes. Put a dash or comma where the word breaks go — for example ULB-SEKY — and I'll find answers that span more than one word.

Are the answers real, or just dictionary words?

Real ones. Every match comes from over 500,000 published Telegraph, Times, Guardian, Independent and Daily Mail clues — never made-up words.

Is it free?

Completely. No sign-up, no ads, and no limit on how many anagrams you solve.
