Crossword pattern matcher — common questions

How do I find a crossword answer with missing letters?

Type the letters you already have and a question mark for each gap — for example S?O?E — then press Search. I return only real crossword answers that fit that shape and length.

What do I type for the unknown letters?

A question mark or a space for each unknown position. Use a dash or comma to show where a multi-word answer breaks.

What does the "Must include" box do?

It narrows results to answers containing particular letters wherever they sit — handy when you have crossing letters but aren't sure of their position.

Are the answers real, and is it free?

Every match comes from over 500,000 published clues, never made-up words — and it's completely free, no sign-up, no ads.
