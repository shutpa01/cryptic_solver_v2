Worth knowing before you start
  1. The match count is invisible in an empty grid by design — it appears at 99 matches or fewer. Type two or three crossing letters to see it in the last square of the entry. A wrong letter gives a red zero.
  2. Tools mode: right-click any square, or use the Tools icon top right. Click words in the clue bar under the grid to feed the anagram and word tools.
  3. Green puzzles below exercise everything; the others have answers but no explanations, so the hint rungs will be greyed out.
